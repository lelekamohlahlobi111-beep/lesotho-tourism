About Lesotho

A small mountain kingdom with big landscapes, warm people and a living culture. Here is what makes it worth the journey.

Who we are

Discover Lesotho is a travel guide built to help visitors plan a trip to the Kingdom of Lesotho.
We bring together the places to see, the things to do and the practical details you need before you arrive.
Our goal is simple: make it easy to find honest, local information and to book the experiences that fit your trip.

Our mission

We want travellers to experience Lesotho in a way that benefits the people who live here. We work with local guides,
family-run lodges and community craft makers, so that tourism income stays in the community.

Questions about planning a trip? Get in touch and we will point you in the right direction.
